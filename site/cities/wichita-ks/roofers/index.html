The work

Repair

A leak, a missing shingle, or flashing that failed. The repair is priced before work starts.

Replacement

A full roof is a separate bid, with the material and the timing written down.

Inspection

A look at the roof, the flashing, and the ceilings inside. You get what was seen, in plain words.

How a job goes

  1. 01Send a ground photo

    The slope, the trouble spot, and a ceiling stain if there is one. Stay off the roof.

  2. 02Book the inspection

    The owner sets a visit. Findings come back as repair, replacement, or no work yet.

  3. 03Approve one path

    You accept a written repair or a written replacement. They are not bundled by surprise.

Before the visit

  • Photos come from the ground.
  • Repair and replacement are separate prices.
  • Storm timing is a visit, not a promise.

Questions

What is in the price?

The written bid names the work, the material, and what is not included.

Should I get on the roof?

No. A photo from the yard is enough to start, if you can take one safely.

Does a storm mean a new roof?

No. An inspection says whether the roof needs a repair, a replacement, or nothing yet.
