The work

Refused items

Paints, fuels, and medical waste stay behind unless the owner has agreed in writing to a specific item.

A load

One full truck, confirmed from photos. More than that is a second load, priced before the day.

Stairs and apartments

Tell them the floor and whether there is an elevator. Access is part of the price.
