The work

Drains and fixtures

Clogs, faucets, toilets, and disposals. A slow drain is not booked as a water-heater job.

Emergency

Active leaks, no water, and a sewer backup. Say what is happening and whether the water is already off.

Water heaters

A repair or a replacement. Have the fuel type, gas or electric, and the age if you know it.

How a job goes

  1. 01Describe what failed

    Leak, clog, no hot water, or a fixture. Say if water is reaching the floor.

  2. 02Take a visit window

    The owner gives a window. This page does not promise a minute.

  3. 03Approve the repair

    Diagnosis comes first. The repair price is written before work past that.

Before the visit

  • Extra work is priced before it starts.
  • Emergency calls and scheduled calls are different.
  • Water-heater jobs ask for fuel type.

Questions

What should I have ready?

The symptom, the address in Peoria, and whether someone will be home.

How is the visit priced?

The owner states the visit terms before rolling, and the repair price before work past the diagnosis.

Should I shut the water off?

If water is reaching the floor and you know the valve, shut it off, then call.
