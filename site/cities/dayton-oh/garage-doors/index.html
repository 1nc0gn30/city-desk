The work

Springs

A loud bang and a heavy door often mean a spring. Do not wind a torsion spring yourself.

Openers

The motor runs and the door does not move, or the remote stopped working. Have the opener brand if you can see it.

Stuck door

It will not open, will not close, or stops halfway. Say what it did just before it failed.

How a job goes

  1. 01Say the symptom

    Stuck, loud bang, or an opener that spins. Stay clear of the springs.

  2. 02Ask for the next visit

    The owner gives an open time. This page does not promise a same-day slot.

  3. 03Approve the part

    Spring, opener, or track. The price is written before the part goes on.

Before the visit

  • The symptom is the first line.
  • Springs are not a homeowner job.
  • Opener work is separate from a spring.

Questions

What should I tell them?

What the door does, whether a car is trapped, and the opener brand if it is visible.

Can I wind the spring?

No. Torsion springs injure people. Wait for the visit.

Do you promise a same-day visit?

No. Ask for the next open visit and take the window you are given.
