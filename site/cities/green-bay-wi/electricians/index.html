The work

Repairs

Outlets, switches, lighting, and a circuit that tripped and will not stay on.

Panels

Questions

Is a flickering light an emergency?

A hot smell, a sparking outlet, or a panel that is warm is a stop-and-call. A single flicker can be a scheduled visit.

Who handles the permit?

The owner tells you if the job needs one, and pulls what the job requires.

Can you price a panel by phone?

No. A panel price follows a look at the equipment.
