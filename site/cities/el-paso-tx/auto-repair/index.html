Questions

Is there a loaner?

Do not assume one. Ask when you book.

What if you cannot do the job?

You are told before the car is booked in, or before a send-out happens.

Do you start work when I drop the car off?

Diagnostics, if you approved them. Repairs wait for the written estimate and your yes.
