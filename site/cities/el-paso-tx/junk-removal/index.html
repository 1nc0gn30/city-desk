The work

A load

One full truck, confirmed from photos. More than that is a second load, priced before the day.

Stairs and apartments

Tell them the floor and whether there is an elevator. Access is part of the price.

Refused items

Paints, fuels, and medical waste stay behind unless the owner has agreed in writing to a specific item.

How a job goes

  1. 01Photograph the pile

    Wide shots and a close shot of anything that is not ordinary furniture or bags.

  2. 02Get a written load price

    It names the truck space and whether stairs are included.

  3. 03Walk it once on the day

    Items added after the photo are priced before they go on the truck.

Before the visit

  • Stairs are disclosed first.
  • Paints, fuels, and medical waste are refused.
  • Photos set the price.

Questions

Do you take paint and fuel?

No. Those stay out of the pile.

What about a walk-up?

Say the floor when you send the photos. Stairs are part of the written price.

What is a load?

One full truck, judged from your photos and confirmed by the owner.
