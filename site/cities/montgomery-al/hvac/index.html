Questions

Is a tune-up a repair?

No. A tune-up is the check. A repair is priced on its own before parts are changed.

When is replacement discussed?

After the equipment has been seen. This page does not price a system from a phone description.

Do I need the filter size?

It helps. If you do not have it, the tech reads it off the old filter.
