The work

Packing

You pack, or the crew packs. The quote says which. Packing materials are listed if the crew brings them.

Local move

Loading, the ride, and unloading at the new address. Both addresses are in the quote.

What the quote needs

Room count, stairs or elevator at each end, heavy pieces, and the date.

How a job goes

  1. 01Send rooms, stairs, and a date

    Add photos of anything very heavy or awkward.

    It names the crew size, the hours or the flat price, and what is not included.

  3. 03Confirm access
