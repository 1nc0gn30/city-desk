The work

Three kinds of job, priced apart

01

Exterior

Siding, trim, doors, and decks this shop paints. Wash, scrape, and caulk happen when the price includes them.

02

Cabinets

Cabinet work is booked apart from a whole-house repaint, so the day is not spent on the wrong job.

03

Interior

Walls, ceilings, trim, and doors. The rooms named in the price are the rooms that get painted.

How a job goes

Three steps, all written down

  1. 01Tell them the rooms

    How many rooms, any photos you have, and the week you want the work.

  2. 02Read the written price

    It names the paint line, the sheen, and the prep that is included.

  3. 03Walk it when it is dry

    You look at the work together. Touch-ups that were in the price get done.

Before the visit

The written price says who buys it, which line, and which sheen.
